My Journey

From Yueyang, China to Albany, New York

Liam Duan grew up in Yueyang, Hunan Province, China, where he was surrounded by art from an early age. Alongside his love for drawing, he was also deeply passionate about basketball and eSports—thriving in competition and teamwork.

A Journey Through Design

After high school, he moved to Albany, New York, drawn by the diversity and creative opportunities in the U.S. During his early years in high school, Liam developed a fascination with digital tools and visual media, which led him to study digital media at a local community college. He later transferred to SUNY Cobleskill, where he specialized in pattern and graphic design.

Shaping the Future

University life was a whirlwind of design projects and volunteer work—from illustrations and logos to poster campaigns. These experiences helped him refine his creativity and strengthen his visual storytelling.

Today, Liam is pursuing his Master’s degree in Information Design and Technology at SUNY Poly, preparing to merge his artistic roots with the evolving world of technology—ready to design with impact in a digital-first era.

My Services

I create visually compelling and brand-consistent designs, including logos, brochures, posters, and marketing materials that communicate messages effectively across both print and digital platforms.

I build responsive, user-friendly websites using HTML, CSS, and Bootstrap. My designs adapt seamlessly to different screen sizes and prioritize clarity, performance, and intuitive navigation.

I craft user-centered digital experiences through wireframes, mockups, and interactive prototypes. My process focuses on usability, accessibility, and design systems that align with user behavior and client goals.
