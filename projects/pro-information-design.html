Data to Visual Stories

Transforming raw data into meaningful visual narratives through structure, storytelling, and interaction.

Overview

Visualizing Information

This project explores how complex data can be transformed into clear and engaging visual narratives using information design principles.

Techniques such as mapping, sequencing, and layering were applied to structure information in a meaningful and accessible way.

The final outcome integrates storytelling and interaction design to enhance audience understanding and engagement.
