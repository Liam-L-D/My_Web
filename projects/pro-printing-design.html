Print Design

Visual Communication Through Print Media

A collection of print design work spanning posters, brochures, editorial layouts, and large-format banners created for communication, promotion, and real-world visual impact.

Overview

Print Design for Real-World Impact

These print projects demonstrate my ability to design visually engaging and professionally formatted materials for a variety of contexts, including community events, school programs, and nonprofit outreach.

I worked across formats such as posters, brochures, editorial spreads, and pull-up banners—each developed to meet production requirements while maintaining clarity, hierarchy, and visual appeal.

The overall focus was on typography, color balance, layout structure, and how printed materials function not just as visuals, but as communication tools in physical space.

Key Design Elements

What this project includes

  • Event posters, flyers, and campaign-oriented print pieces
  • Tri-fold and editorial layouts with structured information hierarchy
  • Large-format banner design prepared for real print production
  • Attention to bleed, resolution, composition, and print readability
