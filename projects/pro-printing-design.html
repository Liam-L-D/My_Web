Visual Communication Through Print Media

Print Design for Real-World Impact

These print projects demonstrate my ability to design visually engaging and professionally formatted materials for a variety of purposes, including community events, school programs, and nonprofit outreach.

I worked across formats such as large posters, folded brochures, and pull-up banners—each carefully developed to meet print production standards while maximizing communication and visual appeal.

My focus was on typography, color balance, spatial layout, and how each element contributes to the audience’s experience both in print and in physical space.

Key Design Elements

  • Event posters, flyers, and cultural campaign prints
  • Tri-fold and bi-fold brochures with structured content hierarchy
  • Large-format signage prepared with bleed, CMYK color, and high-res specs

Tools Used: Adobe Illustrator, InDesign, Photoshop
