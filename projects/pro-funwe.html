Project

Funwe – Brand and Product Identity System

A comprehensive branding project that combines company identity, print design, and product packaging into one unified visual system.

Overview

Brand + Product System

This project presents the comprehensive branding and visual identity system for a conceptual company called Funwe. The design work includes the company’s logo, brand manual, business cards, posters, and more.

Additionally, the project features the visual design of Funwe’s signature product — a card game titled “Secret”. The aim was to create a cohesive and engaging look across both the corporate and product levels.

This project reflects my focus on consistency, creativity, and professional branding execution across multiple formats.

Key Design Elements

What this project includes

  • Visual identity system for a fictional company
  • Logo, posters, business cards, and brand manual design
  • Packaging and visual assets for the “Secret” card game
  • A unified visual language across brand and product materials
