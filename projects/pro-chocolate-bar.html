Packaging Design

Chocolate Bar Packaging

A packaging design project focused on creating a premium chocolate wrapper from concept development to physical prototype.

Overview

Concept + Execution

This project explores the full process of chocolate packaging design, from initial research and concept development to final production.

The design focuses on creating a visually engaging and premium look, while maintaining structural accuracy for real-world packaging.

Through this project, I combined branding, layout design, and hands-on production to deliver a complete packaging solution.

Key Features

What this project includes

  • Custom dieline creation and packaging structure
  • Branding-focused visual design
  • Typography and color system development
  • Physical mockup and prototype production
