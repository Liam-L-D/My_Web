Packaging Design

Chocolate Bar Packaging

A packaging design project focused on creating a premium chocolate wrapper, from concept development and visual direction to dieline preparation and physical prototype production.

Overview

Concept + Execution

This project explores the full process of chocolate packaging design, from initial research and concept development to final production.

The design focuses on creating a visually engaging and premium look, while maintaining structural accuracy for real-world packaging.

Through this project, I combined branding, layout design, typography, color, and hands-on production to deliver a complete packaging solution.

Key Design Elements

What this project includes

  • Custom dieline creation and packaging structure planning
  • Branding-focused visual design for a premium product feel
  • Typography, color, and layout system development
  • Physical mockup and prototype production
