Project

EcoYummy – Edible Utensil Branding

A sustainable branding project exploring edible utensils, eco-friendly packaging, and purpose-driven visual identity design.

Overview

Sustainable Brand Concept

EcoYummy is a conceptual brand created for an undergraduate branding course. The project focuses on edible and eco-friendly utensils as a way to rethink disposable products through design.

This project marked an important step in developing my design identity. I worked through the full branding process, including brainstorming, logo development, moodboard creation, packaging direction, and high-fidelity mockups.

The project helped me build practical branding and prototyping skills while strengthening my belief that design can support environmental responsibility.

Key Design Elements

What this project includes

  • Logo and wordmark system with eco-conscious visual language
  • Packaging direction inspired by natural and biodegradable materials
  • Moodboards exploring color, texture, typography, and brand tone
  • Promotional and digital mockups designed for brand presentation
